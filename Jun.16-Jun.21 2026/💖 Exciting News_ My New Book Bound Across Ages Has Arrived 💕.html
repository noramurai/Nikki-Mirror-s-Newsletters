I'm thrilled to announce that my latest fantasy romance, Bound Across Ages, is now available on Amazon! This epic tale follows a fierce fae warrior and a shifter who is the secret heir to an ancient throne—two souls bound by prophecy yet divided by a centuries-old blood feud between their peoples. When a magical bond forges between these sworn enemies, they must navigate forbidden love while facing a final trial that will determine the fate of both their worlds. Packed with ancient magic, our heroes fight against destiny itself. If you love fantasy romance with high stakes and heart-pounding passion, Bound Across Ages delivers everything you crave in one unforgettable story.

I hope with all my heart that you like it and enjoy it. 

I go on an epic quest through ancient magical times in order to save his soul.

I've spent years mastering powerful spells that made sorcerers whisper my name in awe.

Nothing prepared me for the way my powers surge toward Professor Kael.

The first time I entered the Academy, I felt this tremendous pull. I knew it was him.

A tide I can't control. My heart races when he looks at me.

He was cursed nine years ago for defying time.

This left him guarded; yet, his passionate soul is there.

The attraction between us grows more and more with every moment we spend together.

The prophecy is clear: only the sole one can heal him, and we have less than a year before his torment becomes eternal.

I must follow him through time's forbidden gates to save the soul of the man I'm falling for. But those who dare to manipulate time rarely return whole, if they return at all.
