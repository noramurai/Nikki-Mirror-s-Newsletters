When a chaotic, sharp-witted ADHD sky-courier and a grumpy investigator get stranded for three days on a mysterious "ghost" island that shouldn't exist, sparks fly and a conspiracy unravels. Complete with the perfect fated-mates pull, an irresistible "he falls first" romance, and delicious open-door heat, this story proves that while nothing is fixed, everything can be found.


She's an ADHD sky-courier who files reports no one reads. He's been sent to confirm a ghost island doesn't exist. The island has ruins, warm stone, and a conspiracy that should have stayed buried.

Stranded together for three days with no way off, she maps the truth in charcoal and he falls for the way her mind moves. An ancient bond hums between them. Neither of them asked for it. Neither of them is walking away.

Grumpy/sunshine. Fated mates. He falls first. Open door. ADHD named on page.
