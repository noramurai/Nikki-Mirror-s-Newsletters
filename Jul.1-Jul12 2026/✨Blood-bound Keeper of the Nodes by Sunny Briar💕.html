I thought reactivating the Convergence Mark nodes and halting the Council’s purge would fulfill my destiny and let me build a normal life with my fated mate, Caden. Turns out I had it all wrong.

When the Council wiped out the hybrid practitioners who maintained the network, Vashara from my bloodline dove into the subterranean trench and became its living anchor. For 300 years, she held it alone, awaiting a successor, while generations of our people were killed.

With her life reserves drained, Vashara is holding on by sheer willpower, and the network is starting to strain. When my mother sacrificed herself to save me, the blood oath passed to me: take the anchor duties or let the coastal communities die. But there is a catch.

To complete the transfer, Caden must descend seventy meters to the shelf floor and feed his lightning current into me. This is an impossible dive for an atmospheric being whose lungs and body will be crushed and poisoned by the pressure of deep water.

With the Council racing to deploy a suppression field, I have hours to save the coast and protect the man I love before I lose them both.
