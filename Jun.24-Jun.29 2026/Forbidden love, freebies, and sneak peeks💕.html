From Global Award-Winning author KC Wilde

Pinehaven, a town no longer dying but reborn under the eternal protection of the FAFO Wolves

There are things buried under the trees. Things you do Not want to know about. Dangers far greater than anyone can imagine are buried deep within the whispering woods.

Pinehaven isn't just another town; it's a sanctuary with a thriving casino, and growing population led by a stubborn mayor with Nightshade blood and a fiercely protective Alpha by her side.
People are starting to notice that Pinehaven survives things other towns do not.
Hunters still circle. Politicians still lie.

Humans and wolves alike are learning what it means to choose each other across every line that should keep them apart.
Many trying to turn a lifetime of guilt into something that looks like home.

The line has been drawn in the dirt.

Anyone who tries to come for its people is about to learn exactly who has been watching from the woods.

If you love thrilling paranormal romance with strong alphas, resilient heroines, and a fight for survival, then dive into FAFO Wolves: Pinehaven. The Shifter Town
