🔥Can fate bring two wounded hearts together? When Lauren, a professional wish-granter, crosses paths with a handsome doctor and his adorable daughter, an unexpected journey of love, healing, and second chances begins. Send Wish to World by Rachel J. Green is a heartwarming single-dad romance that proves sometimes the greatest wishes are the ones we never knew we needed.❤️


If you weren't a believer in fate before, you will be after reading this story. Will a new chance lead to true love?

Lauren is excellent at her job. She works at the online company, SendWishToWorld.com. Her job is to try her best to fulfill wishes.

It all started when a moody and rude Lauren collapsed in the middle of the road. She ends up in the hospital where she meets an entirely inaccessible sexy and hot Dr. Jones. After meeting, fate intervenes to put everything on the right track.

An adorable little girl, Riley, comes with her wish to make her dad happy again.

Will Riley's wish come true?

Send Wish to World is a single dad romance that will make you believe in the power of fate. A sweet and emotional contemporary romance about love after loss and a second chance.

If you like drama, romance, tension, and suspense, you will love this series from Rachel J. Green.
