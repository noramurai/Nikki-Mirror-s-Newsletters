✨Sold by her own father. Married to a monster. Addicted to his touch. 🔥 In His Grip, a ruthless Bratva king and his unwilling bride ignite a dangerous obsession where passion and betrayal collide. Packed with deadly secrets, forbidden desire, and scorching chemistry, this dark mafia romance will leave you breathless and craving more.


He's a monster who bought me with blood money. Now I am trapped and worst of all... I crave this monster.

Sold to him by my own father, I became Viktor's wife.
And nowI married the most dangerous man in New York.
I should hate Viktor. I should fear him. But...

Every time he looks at me and touches me, I fall deeper into his arms. He is like an addiction; you need to get rid of him but really don't want to. His eyes. His touch. His dominance.

It wasn’t love. It wasn’t a choice. Now, it’s turning into a sick obsession.

Viktor hides dark, deadly secrets—secrets that could destroy the Bratva empire.

But so do I....

Will I let him destroy me, or will I destroy him? Which one will it be?

His Grip is an intense dark romance filled with obsession, passion, and danger.
