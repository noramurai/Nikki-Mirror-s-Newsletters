Lin Bai was hired to solve an impossible refrigeration problem.
She wasn't hired to diagnose a dragon.
Morgan MacCrain has spent three years working nights inside a failing cold-storage plant, where brutal temperatures keep his failing fire under control. He's big, quiet, dangerously private—and determined to keep the brilliant engineer investigating his building from discovering what he really is.
But Halvorsen Cold Storage makes no sense.
Temperatures plunge without explanation. Equipment fails. Morgan handles frozen steel with bare hands. And Lin becomes certain the plant isn't simply losing energy.
Something is taking it.
The trail leads to an abandoned brick ice house attached to the modern plant—and a secret the Halvorsen family has hidden for generations.
Now Morgan may be its next victim.
Saving him means trusting a dragon who's spent decades hiding from the world. Stopping the Halvorsens could cost Morgan the fire he has left.
Or his life.
Some things were never meant to survive the cold.
Some dragons do anyway.
