Get ready to fall for Tempted by Moonlight, a high-stakes, fated-mates story packed with ancient magic, pack politics, and a bond hot enough to turn resistance to ash.


I was an Irish witch with forbidden moonlight magic and a hellhound, I call friend.
The forest delivered me to the one wolf I should never touch.

Jace Calloway was trouble in leather and fangs. He hauled me back to his pack like I belonged there. He strutted around shirtless as if the moon paid him rent.

I smiled, lied, and kept my mission tucked under my violet gown. His Dirty Dare bar gave me a job and a bed.

His hands on my hips made my spells stutter. His mouth on mine turned resistance into ash.

But, no way is he the Alpha of prophecy who will help me defeat evil and save my world. He says he can and will.

Yet, if we can’t defeat the evil that followed me, my coven, our love, and the two of us will die. Oops! I mean the three of us.
