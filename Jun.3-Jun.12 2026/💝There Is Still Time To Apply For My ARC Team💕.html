✨Ready for a steamy, high-stakes fantasy romance filled with forced proximity and explosive magical bonds? Dive into Fated to the Storm King, an electrifying fated-mates story where a deadly tournament pits a fierce heroine against the ruthless king she’s magically bound to hate, or burn for.


I blew up a city block with lightning—and now I’m magically bound to the king who killed my grandmother.

Draven Blackthorn is ruthless and impossibly powerful.
He drags me into his world like a storm—demanding, dominant, and devastating.

I’m supposed to hate him.
Instead, I burn for him.
We fight. We train. We touch.
And every time we do, the sparks turn dangerous.

Now I’m the Storm Court’s champion in a tournament rigged to kill me.
Draven’s the only reason I’ve survived this long.
But he’s also the reason I’m losing control.
Of my magic. Of my body. Of everything I thought I knew.

The bond between us is electric, and impossible to ignore.
I try to stay away, but we keep crossing lines.
Always reaching for each other like we’re starving.

Then I learn the final trial pits me against him.
One crown. One winner.

And if I survive, it won’t be as the girl I was.
Because I’ve already let him ruin me—in every way that counts.
