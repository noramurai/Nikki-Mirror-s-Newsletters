✨When a second chance at a forgotten one-night stand turns out to be her cold, off-limits new Alpha boss, tension explodes as they find themselves trapped together in a mountain storm. But as an addictive attraction grows, she must hide the ultimate secret: the son, he knows nothing about, carries his very same eyes.

I take a job from my one-night stand, and my son’s father.

I drive up a mountain ready to prove myself. Then, I bump into the man I can’t forget.

I never mention the son with his eyes.

He’s my new boss: cold, controlled, off-limits, and my body responds to him.

We're stuck in a storm, arguing about every choice.

He brings coffee at 3 a.m. and treats people as if they matter.

Then I learn the truth; he’s an Alpha. And my blood doesn’t obey.

I tell myself it’s closeness and tension, only unfinished business.

Then he touches me as if he’s already claimed me and looks at me as if he has fallen for me.

If he discovers my son is his, I could lose him forever.
