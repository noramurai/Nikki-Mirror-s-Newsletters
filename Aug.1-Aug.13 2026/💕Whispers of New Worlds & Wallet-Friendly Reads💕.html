✨If you are obsessed with dark, high-steam romance, calculated billionaire alphas, and deliciously toxic power dynamics, you are going to fall hard for this latest read. Meet Sam: handsome, brilliant, and utterly controlling, except his "accidental" entrance into her life was completely engineered, and every irresistible moment has been meticulously planned.

Check out the blurb below to see if you can resist his control, or if you'd get pulled right into the whirlpool.


A dark stalker romance full of obsession. One of those toxic loves you need to get rid of, but you can't.

He's been watching me. All this time.

I thought meeting Sam was fate—a chance encounter that sparked into something electric. Imagine randomly meeting a grippingly handsome, brilliant tech billionaire who is into you. He seemed perfect. Too perfect.

He is dominant, passionate, and...controlling. Not like you think "controlling". No. He controls the number of times he brings me over the edge. I cannot say I don't like it, though; I love it. And this is what scares me. He is introducing me to an entirely new world of dominance and pleasure.

I found myself desperately craving his control... until I discovered the truth.

Sam didn't just accidentally enter my life. He engineered the whole meeting.

Every "coincidence" was calculated. Every moment is orchestrated. Everything was planned long before I knew his name.

Now, I'm torn between the burning desire of his control and the terrifying reality of who he really is. A stalker. He is my stalker. The deeper I fall, the darker the secrets become.

But here's the most dangerous truth of all: Part of me doesn't want to escape.

What should I do? Leave him or stay in this whirlpool of toxic love? What would you do?
