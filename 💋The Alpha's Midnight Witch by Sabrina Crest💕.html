I thought I was a normal woman with a Halloween problem. Turns out I'm a witch, and the gorgeous neighbor I've been fantasizing about for six months is a wolf.

Gage Corbett is huge, tattooed, growly, and so gorgeous I sometimes forget we’re only friends.
Then weird things start happening, my mirrors whisper my name, and a creature tries to kill me in my backyard.

Gage saves me by turning into a massive wolf right in front of me. Shocker!

Then, I find out I'm a witch and two covens are hunting me for my special powers.

All of a sudden there’s danger everywhere and he’s turned into my protector.
Now I’m stuck under his roof while he teaches me how to control the magic I have no idea how to use.
Somewhere between magical disasters, shared breakfasts, and very bad decisions, I fall hopelessly in love with him.

And his wolf thinks I’m his fated mate.

But, at midnight on Halloween, when I inherit my magic, I still have no idea what I'll turn into when it's over.
