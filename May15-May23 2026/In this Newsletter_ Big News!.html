✨One forensic auditor with a past, one massive enforcer with a job to do, and $2.3 million in phantom funds that were never meant to be found. In this audit of the Collegia Noctis, the numbers are more lethal than the men sent to stop them.

The Collegia Noctis has been burying money for 150 years.

The Ashcroft Preservation Fund is the visible mechanism — an endowment that funds scholarships, maintains the buildings, and, somewhere beneath the surface, moves $2.3 million into accounts that don't exist on any public filing. Seven subsidiary entities. Seven connected institutions. A disbursement code nobody can explain.

The reformed Council hired Tess Calloway to open the books.

She's twenty-eight. Forensic auditor. Daughter of a man currently serving seven years at FCI Danbury for a nearly identical crime. She counts everything — steps, seconds, the line weights of her mechanical pencils. In nine years of auditing institutions that didn't want to be audited, she has never been stopped.

They assigned the Advocate to keep her alive.

Cain Marchetti is 6'4", 230 pounds, and learned early that size is a language. He defends merit members in the Collegia's trials with a Southie Boston accent he never polished out. He's never been asked to stand guard over an accountant.

He's also never been read as precisely as she reads him the moment she walks into the conference room.

"The numbers are going to hurt more than your fists."

She says it in their first conversation.

He believes her.
