✨And now get ready for the ultimate forced proximity in this cozy monster romance where an infuriating kraken ex-husband is forced to re-bind his magic to his ex-wife—or watch her die. Packed with rogue tentacle spice, bitter enemies-to-lovers energy, and a seriously groveling sea monster, this fated-mates story is a must-read.

I swore I’d rather marry a sentient rock than be spiritually tied to my kraken ex-husband again. The town council disagreed.


I barely survived my first Union Vow to Jin Hai—the infuriatingly guarded sea monster who broke my heart, calling our short marriage "just a medical contract" before calling the whole thing off. But when a magical crisis threatens to burn me out, the Council gives me an ultimatum: Re-bind my magic with the ex who discarded me, or die.

The Registry whispers our magic makes us a fated True Match. I just think he's a jerk

Now I’m trapped back on his cramped ship for sixty days. My strategy? Weaponized competence. Treat him strictly like a coworker, fix the failing sea-wards, and never let him see me sweat or cry ever again.

But the cold bastard who pushed me away is suddenly hovering. He tries to act icy, but his massive tentacles apparently have a mind of their own—and they are constantly, desperately trying to pull me close.

As sabotaged magic forces us into tight quarters and "medically necessary" embraces, I'm realizing the scariest threat isn't lurking in the deep. It's discovering I never stopped wanting him.

60 More Days with the Kraken is a cozy monster romance featuring a medically necessary arranged marriage, reluctant fated mates, bitter enemies-to-lovers exes, forced proximity on a cramped ship, a groveling kraken, rogue tentacle spice, and open-door heat on the way to a fiercely domestic HEA.
