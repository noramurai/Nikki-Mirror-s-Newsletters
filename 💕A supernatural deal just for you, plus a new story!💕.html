✨She married him for power. He married her for control. Neither expected to meet their match. In a ruthless world of betrayal, vengeance, and forbidden desire, two dangerous enemies become each other’s greatest weakness. But when blood is spilled and secrets unravel, will their passion save them or destroy everything?


Obsession. Betrayal. Revenge. And not your typical damsel in distress.

Savina: An arranged marriage to Andres Valdez, heir to a ruthless Colombian empire, is meant to secure peace. But in my world, where vows are sealed in blood, I never wanted this union. I built my father's empire from the ground up, and no one dictates my path. Andres is arrogant, cold, and reckless—everything I despise. Yet, as Russian and Irish vultures circle, our forced alliance ignites a dangerous game. He crossed a line, and I reminded him I have teeth. I'm here to finish what began the night they killed my family. But somewhere along the way, hatred twisted into a lustful craving that I can't deny. And that might be the most dangerous part of all.

Andres: The moment I saw Savina, I knew she wouldn't bend. She's no mere mafia princess; she's a queen who fights back harder than expected. Every public humiliation, every move behind my back, every insult—it only made me want her more. To top it all off, she is now my wife. I didn't want a wife. I wanted leverage. But she's the only one who sees through me, the only one I'd kill to protect. I thought I was in control. Now, I'd burn my world down if someone laid a hand on her. But the funny thing is, she doesn't need my protection.
