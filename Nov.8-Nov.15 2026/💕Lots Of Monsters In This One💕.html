✨A mysterious portal, a dangerously sexy wolf king, and an irresistible fated-mate bond! Fated to the Wolf King is a steamy fantasy romance packed with forbidden passion, ancient magic, and thrilling danger.


All I want is a little solitude at my weekend cabin.
What I get instead is a portal to another world.

Ruled by giant wolves that not only speak, but walk on two legs.

And the moment I try to get back home, an attractive six-foot mountain of muscle with blue-grey eyes and hands like vice grips slings me over one shoulder and carries me off to his den.

Rylan Vansant is an arrogant, brutally dangerous wolf with a sharp tongue and enough enemies to fill a graveyard.

I should have run the second I had the chance.

But it turns out he's not just any wolf.
He's also my fated mate.

And the only thing keeping me alive.

I spent the last three months trying to bury a broken heart.
He spent the last three decades trying to bury a broken realm.

As proximity and danger collide, our forbidden bond quickly catches fire, removing denial one layer at a time.

He tries to outrun it.
I try to ignore it.
Neither of us stands a chance.

As dormant powers begin to awaken in me, a powerful enemy emerges from the shadows, devouring everything in its path.

Will I learn to use my powers before it's too late…

…or will the last wolf king die before he has a chance to put on the crown?

Fated to the Wolf King is a steamy wolf-shifter fantasy romance featuring fated mates, forced proximity, touch-her-and-die energy, a possessive alpha, ancient magic, a lost royal heir, and a fierce heroine awakening to a power that could change both their worlds.

Content Warning: Contains strong language and sexual situations.
