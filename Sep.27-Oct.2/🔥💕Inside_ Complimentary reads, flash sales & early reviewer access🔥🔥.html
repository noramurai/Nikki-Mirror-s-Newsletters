Featured Today

✨A love that never truly disappeared gets a second chance in this emotional small-town romance. When a mountain rescue brings a successful Denver attorney face-to-face with Sheriff Bobby, the man who broke her heart fourteen years ago, old wounds, buried longing, and undeniable chemistry come rushing back. Can they finally confront the past and find their way home to each other?


Sheriff Bobby, the man I knew in college as ‘Roberto,’ is an enigma.

Something happened to him in Mountain View Springs, something that broke him. But in Silver Pine, he found acceptance, he found a new life.

On a day hike, I get lost in the mountains. He is the only man who knows me, who can find me, and save me as hyperthermia begins to set in.

Fourteen years ago, he chose to walk away from me, just after college, thinking that he needed to choose for me, that he needed to protect me.

Being alone cleared a pathway through law school, and to a distinguished Denver corporate law firm. Being alone also opened up a long period of loneliness, leaving a void I tried to fill with long hours, ambition, and promotions.

Now I find myself close to him again, the man who knows me perhaps better than I know myself. He is the man I never stopped loving, the man in the center of my dreams. The man my body aches for.

Can we find a pathway ‘home’? Can we overcome the pain of our past, and the trauma that ensued after he walked away, to find a pathway forward?
