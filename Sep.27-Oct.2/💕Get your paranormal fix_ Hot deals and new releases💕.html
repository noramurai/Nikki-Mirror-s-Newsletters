✨Looking for a dark, dangerously addictive mafia romance? Claimed by Him delivers a possessive billionaire mafia boss, forced proximity, enemies-to-lovers tension, and plenty of heat. When a housekeeper is mistaken for a spy by the ruthless Damian Blackwood, suspicion quickly turns into obsession, and escape may be the last thing she wants. A steamy standalone for readers who enjoy dark romance, captive dynamics, and an intense happily ever after.


I'm the maid, and he is the most dangerous mafia billionaire.
He thinks I'm a spy. What does he do with traitors? Captures them.

When I took the job as Damian Blackwood's housekeeper.
I thought I'd finally found a safe place. I was wrong.

Those ice-blue eyes watch my every move.
His suspicious mind twisted my presence into a threat.
Until one night when suspicion turns into obsession.

And he decides to take matters into his own hands.

Now I'm trapped in his penthouse,
Subject to his darkest desires and demanding questions.
He won't stop until he gives me pleasure and pain.
And I don't want him to stop.

Get ready for a dangerously hot mafia romance that will keep you hooked. Perfect for fans of dark, obsessive alpha males and fiery enemies-to-lovers tension.

CLAIMED BY HIM is a standalone dark mafia romance packed with:
✔️ A possessive billionaire mafia boss
✔️ Forced proximity & captive romance
✔️ Explosive, steamy scenes
✔️ Guaranteed HEA
