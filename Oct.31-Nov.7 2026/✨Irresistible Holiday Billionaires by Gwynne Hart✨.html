Four billionaire grumps.
Four women who refuse to play by their rules.
One Christmas season that's about to get very hot.

From snowed-in enemies to fake lovers who can't keep their hands off each other, these powerful men are about to discover the one thing money can't buy. This box set, each a standalone with a HEA, includes:

  • Snowed in with the Billionaire Grump
  • Christmas Twist
  • Christmas Challenge with the Enemy Grump
  • Silver Fox Billionaire's Holidate

---

Snowed in with the Billionaire Grump


Five years ago, he fired the brilliant woman who dared defy him.
Now he wants her groundbreaking tech—and the woman he never forgot.

When a Christmas Eve snowstorm traps them together, their old arguments turn into irresistible temptation. One scorching night is all it takes to remind him why he's never been able to get her out of his head.

He's secretly arranged the funding that could save her startup. But if she discovers he's been pulling the strings, he could lose the woman he's finally ready to love.

---

Christmas Twist


She fought his shipping empire to save the whales.
Now her silver fox billionaire enemy wants her to marry him.

His mother's will demands a wife and twenty-four acts of service by Christmas Eve. He offers the environmental changes she's been fighting for, and she agrees. But their tiny cottage has one bed, and the chalk line she draws across the floor doesn't stand a chance against their chemistry.

She's discovering a man beneath the arrogant billionaire who makes her want far more than a marriage contract. But he's hiding a grief he won't share, and she can't build a future with a man who refuses to let her in.

---

Christmas Challenge with the Enemy Grump


One unforgettable night with a silver fox ex-SEAL leaves her pregnant.
Then she starts her new job—and there he is.

Now they're racing to reopen the city's Winter Market in eighteen days. She wants Christmas magic. He wants security and control. Their arguments are practically foreplay, and one late night against his desk proves neither has forgotten their first encounter.

She's falling for the man behind the grump, but he keeps trying to run her life as if she's another mission to manage. If he can't learn to trust her, he'll lose more than the woman he loves. He'll lose his chance at the family neither of them expected.

Trigger warning: Christmas Challenge with the Enemy Grump is a standalone enemies-to-lovers Christmas romance with a Happily Ever After ending.

But along the way, the characters deal with military PTSD nightmares, a crowd crush that resulted in deaths, and grief from a loved one's death from cancer.

---

Silver Fox Billionaire's Holidate


She's his bodyguard, not his girlfriend.
But fake dating her gorgeous billionaire client on national television is becoming a very dangerous game.

A televised Christmas couples competition could rescue the grieving widower's public reputation. She agrees to play his girlfriend while keeping him and his son safe. But when his thumb circles her pulse, their staged kisses turn real—and neither wants to stop when the cameras do.

Then a security scare exposes their secret, turning his fake romance into a public scandal. Just when they've found something worth fighting for, the world threatens to tear them apart.
