Thirty days to find a husband, or I lose my grandmother’s restaurant.

The only man rich enough to save it? The billionaire who’s spent two years trying to bulldoze it.
So I offered my enemy a deal. One year. Fake marriage. No feelings, no touching that means a thing.
Now Dominic Vale sleeps in the apartment above my kitchen. Shares my only bathroom. And has to convince all of New York he’s wild about me.
He’s arrogant. He’s infuriating. He prices my whole life in a single glance.
So why can’t I stop counting the seconds until he looks at me like he means it?
But my husband is hiding something. A secret with a wrecking ball at the end of it.
And when I finally learn the truth, I won’t know if the man in my bed is here to save my whole world.
Or to tear it down.
For Better or For Business is a steamy, standalone marriage-of-convenience romance — grumpy billionaire, fierce chef, enemies to lovers, forced proximity, and only one bathroom. Guaranteed happily ever after. No cheating, no cliffhanger.
