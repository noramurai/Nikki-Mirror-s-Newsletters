Featured Today

✨Power, ambition, and irresistible chemistry collide in this billionaire romance. When a fiercely independent attorney goes head-to-head with a Greek shipping magnate, professional rivalry quickly turns dangerously personal. Add Athens, London, family secrets, and plenty of temptation, and you have a romance where surrender may be the biggest risk of all.


He built his empire on control.
She’s the one woman willing to challenge it.
Maritime attorney Faith Stavros has spent her career proving she belongs in rooms dominated by powerful men. So when a catastrophic port safety failure throws her into the path of Greek shipping billionaire Nikos Papadopoulos, she refuses to be intimidated by his wealth, his influence, or the dangerous intensity behind his dark eyes.
Nikos is fighting to modernize his family’s shipping empire while battling relentless pressure from his father, his board, and the legacy threatening to consume him. The last thing he needs is a brilliant attorney exposing weaknesses in his ambitious eco-fleet project, especially one who unsettles him with every sharp word and stolen glance.
Forced to work together between Athens, London, and the high stakes world of international shipping, their rivalry ignites into something far more dangerous. The closer they get, the harder it becomes to separate professional duty from undeniable desire.
But when loyalty, ethics, and betrayal collide, Faith and Nikos are forced to decide what matters more, protecting their careers or risking everything for a love neither of them expected.
Because some promises can destroy you.
And some are worth surrendering everything to keep.
