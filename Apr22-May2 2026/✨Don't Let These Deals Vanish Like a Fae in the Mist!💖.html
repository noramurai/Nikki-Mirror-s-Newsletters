The Moon Goddess has been guiding my path since birth—leading me to my mate… and to the place I was always meant to stand.

It started as a simple Wolf Council assignment—register a pack in the Catskills. My mate isn’t thrilled about me going alone, but he knows better than to argue.

The moment I arrive, I know something’s off. Their Alpha’s aura isn’t whole. I don’t wait to figure it out alone. I call Luke in.

When he gets there, the way the pack looks at us changes. Not cautious. Not curious. Like they recognize something they’ve been waiting for.

And then everything goes wrong.
I’m captured, locked in a box, and thrown into the bottom of the lake.

Luke saves me just in time.

But someone in this pack wanted me dead… and they’re about to find out what happens when my mate gets involved.
