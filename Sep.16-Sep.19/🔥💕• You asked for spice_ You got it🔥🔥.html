✨She came to Hidden Harbor to escape her past, but she didn’t count on the boy who once held her heart coming back into her life. Packed with small-town nostalgia, pulse-pounding danger, and a fierce hero who will stop at nothing to protect the woman he loves, this romantic suspense will keep you hooked until the very last page!


Lily
I arrived in Hidden Harbor to disappear. New name. New life. No looking back.
Then James Stewart was the childhood heartthrob I never forgot, walked back into my life. He’s not the shy, quiet boy I remember. He asks too many questions and sees too much. And he makes me want more than I can afford to want.
Because the past I escaped is closing in, and if I’m found, the stakes are higher than ever, It could cost me everything.

James
Lily thinks she can keep running. She’s wrong.
The deeper I dig, the more I uncover. Hidden money, dangerous alliances, two powerful families, and a man who believes she still belongs to him. I’ll uncover every secret and take on anyone who threatens her.
I’ll burn the world down for her if I have to. I will not lose her again.
