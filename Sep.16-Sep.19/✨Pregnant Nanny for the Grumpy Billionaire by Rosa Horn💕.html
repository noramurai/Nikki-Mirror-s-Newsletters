I’m pregnant by my grumpy billionaire boss.

His five-year-old thinks I’m just the nanny.

Now her daddy wants me to fake being his girlfriend.

Callum Hawthorne was one reckless night in a tux.

Now I sleep down the hall from that mouth I still remember.

He finds my prenatal vitamins, I expect war.

He offers a deal, fake date him until his takeover vote closes.

Zero feelings.

He runs his empire on black coffee and grief.

Then he kneels to tie his daughter’s shoes.

Leaves the hall light on when she’s scared.

And looks at me like restraint hurts.

Then his hand settles on my waist at the gala, and his mouth finds mine when nobody’s watching.

Between bedtime stories and stolen kisses, pretending feels like family.

But the vote is almost over, soon Callum won’t need me.

And I’m terrified the only fake thing between us…

…was the future I thought we were building.
