✨Two fated couples. One rogue dragon tearing through history. Zero mercy. Fated to the Fallen Wolf is out now, bringing you explosive heat, time-travel stakes, and two Alphas who will fight time itself to protect their mates and unborn child. Dive into this emotional, steamy shifter romance now before history gets rewritten!


They fought fate to find each other. Now they must fight time itself to stay together.

Mael Griffin and Sacha Cadell thought their love had already survived the impossible. Drake Blackwood and Lila Ravensong broke a love curse that was never supposed to let them have a future. Now Lila is carrying their child, Sacha has finally found the family she never thought she would have, and both couples are beginning to believe they’ve earned their happily ever after.

Until a dragon shifter escapes—and begins rewriting the past.

Thrown centuries into the past, they discover a forbidden love between enemy bloodlines—and a history far more connected to their own than any of them imagined. Now they must protect a bond the world was determined to destroy... before changing the past costs them everything they fought for in the present.

For Mael, the past brings him face-to-face with the father whose darkness has haunted him for centuries, forcing Sacha to fight for the man she loves when he begins questioning everything he thought he knew about himself. For Sacha, it means discovering the family and roots she spent a lifetime believing she never had—and choosing, all over again, the mate who became her home.

For Drake and Lila, the stakes are even more intimate. The heat between them has never faded, but now the passion that once defied a curse has become something deeper—a bond built on love, desire, and the family they created together. On the verge of becoming parents, they must protect not only their love, but the bloodline that will lead to their unborn child. And as they uncover the forbidden love that began it all, they finally understand why fate fought so hard to bring them together.

Two fated-mate bonds. One forbidden love lost to history. And an enemy willing to rewrite time to erase them all.

Fated to the Fallen Wolf is a steamy, emotional wolf-shifter romance filled with fated mates, forbidden love, powerful Alphas, ancient bloodlines, family, sacrifice, time travel, and couples who will defy fate, history, and death itself for the ones they love.
