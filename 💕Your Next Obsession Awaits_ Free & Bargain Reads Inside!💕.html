✨If you're a fan of high-stakes dark mafia romance, you won't want to miss The Bratva's Enemy Bride by Eva Lush. This intense, explicit enemies-to-lovers story follows Leonid Bugrov, a former syndicate member turned empire-head who takes Natasha Karamazov, the daughter of his rival, as his arranged bride to settle an old debt, only to find neither of them can resist the fire between them.


Leonid Bugrov was part of the Bratva—until they betrayed him. He came back for revenge and took their empire. Now he wants the one thing still out of reach: Natasha.

Natasha: One night, no names. That’s all it was supposed to be... Now I wear his ring. Sleep in his bed. I know he has blood on his hands, maybe even my father’s. But the way he takes me—slow, then savage—makes me forget why I ever resisted.

Leonid: One night, no names. Then I learned that she was Karamazov’s daughter. I married her to settle the score. But now, every time she moans my name, I know—no one will ever take her from me.

Dark mafia romance books have never been this explicit

“The Bratva’s Enemy Bride” is a dark mafia arranged marriage novel with a haunting enemies-to-lovers twist. One of those bratva mafia romance books that feels a little too real, a little too close—and that’s exactly why you can’t stop turning the pages.

Part of the “Nikitin Bratva” Russian mafia books series by Eva Lush.
