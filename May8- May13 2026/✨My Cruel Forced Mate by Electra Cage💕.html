This second-chance paranormal romance follows a powerful witch forced back into the arms of the Alpha who once rejected her. As old wounds are reopened and a fated bond takes hold, she must decide if she can truly surrender to the man who broke her heart💕

The Alpha humiliated and rejected me. Years later, I’m forced to mate with him.

He bullied me by day and took my virginity at night, unlocking my forbidden magical powers.
He rejected me after, and as a bonus, got me banished for being a dirty witch.
Years later, he’s an elite ops and Alpha, and he forces me back to our pack to marry him.

It’s been years, but I still judge myself for letting someone so cruel take something so holy from me.
I’ve gotten stronger, though, and I now work at a paranormal division studying demon activity.
But when I get caught in a demon swarm, the Alpha from my past marches in to save me.
His wolf roars to life and kidnaps me back to the pack of my youth to claim me as his mate.

I vow that I will never be his again, not after the way he tore my heart in two.
But he forces me to confront all the hurt that I’ve worked so hard to suppress.
He binds me to him by marriage, and his hands are opening every wound on my big body.
And slowly, I soften. Unwillingly, I succumb. Unaware, I let go and let him heal me.

Is my pregnant body ready to surrender to the Alpha?

The Pine Creek wolves are commanding, protective, and possessive to the bone. They’re arrogant enough to think that they can get anyone and anything they want. And they’re obsessed enough to know that they only want one thing: Their true mate.
