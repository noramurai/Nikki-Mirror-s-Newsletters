✨She’s running for her life. He’s the Alpha determined to save it.A human on the run, she’s spent years surviving in the shadows—and she definitely doesn’t believe in werewolves or fated mates. But when deadly enemies close in, a dangerously protective alpha wolf steps between her and the hunt. He doesn't command her; he protects her, sparking a primal, slow-burn heat that neither of them can deny. Staying makes his pack a target, but leaving means losing the only place she's ever felt safe. Dive into this high-stakes, emotionally charged standalone romance in the Blackridge Pack series, and find out if a bond this intense can survive a war.Grab your copy of Fated to the Protective Alpha today!


I’m a human on the run.

He’s a wolf shifter who says I’m his mate.

The men who want me dead know where I am.
And they’re coming for me.

I don’t believe in werewolves.

Or alphas.

And I definitely don’t believe in some primal bond tying me to a dangerous stranger who looks at me like I already belong to him.

I’ve spent years surviving on my own.

I know how to run.
I know how to hide.

I know better than to trust a man who makes my body crave and my instincts panic.

But he doesn’t cage me.
He doesn’t command me.
He protects me.

And the worst part?

I feel it too.

That pull.
That heat.
That dangerous need to stay.

But the men hunting me just changed the rules.
And protecting me is pulling his pack into a fight they can’t win.

If I stay, I make him a target.

If I leave, I lose the only place that’s ever felt safe.

And if I let him claim me?

There’s no escaping what we become.

This slow burn to steamy wolf shifters romance delivers fated mates, a human on the run, and a dangerously protective alpha wolf who won’t let her go. Expect intense emotional tension, deadly enemies closing in, and a bond that puts everything at risk.

Guaranteed HEA. Standalone that is part of the Blackridge Pack series. Each book follows a new couple, with interconnected characters across the series.
